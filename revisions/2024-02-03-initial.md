# Revision: Initial Document Creation

**Date:** February 3, 2024
**Type:** Initial revision

## What Changed

This is the first version of the living document. The initial structure includes three main sections:
- **Introduction** - Explaining the concept of a living document
- **Core Ideas** - Two fundamental principles: revision as feature and transparency through history
- **Open Questions** - Unresolved questions about the approach

## Why

I wanted to create a framework for tracking my evolving understanding of ideas over time. Rather than writing disconnected blog posts or essays that become outdated, this approach maintains a coherent current synthesis while preserving the history of how my thinking developed.

The living document philosophy emerged from frustration with traditional publishing models:
- Old blog posts that I no longer agree with but remain online
- Difficulty maintaining a consistent viewpoint across many writings
- Lack of transparency about how ideas evolved

By combining Git's version control with explanatory essays for each revision, this system makes intellectual evolution both visible and understandable.

## Previous Thinking

This is the first revision, so there's no previous version to compare to. However, the ideas here build on:
- Personal knowledge management systems (Zettelkasten, digital gardens)
- Working in public / learning in public movements
- Academic traditions of revised editions and errata
- Software development practices (version control, changelogs)

## Open Questions

This initial version raises several questions:

1. **Revision Frequency**: How often should significant revisions happen? Daily? Monthly? Only when major insights emerge?

2. **Granularity**: What level of change warrants a new revision entry versus just updating the document quietly?

3. **Commentary Depth**: How detailed should revision essays be? Full essays or brief notes?

4. **Multiple Documents**: Should there be one living document or multiple interconnected ones?

5. **Collaboration**: Can this model work with multiple authors, or is it inherently personal?

## Next Steps

Future revisions will likely:
- Add concrete examples of how specific ideas evolved

- Experiment with linking between different sections across time
- Consider adding a citation/sources layer

The document is intentionally starting simple to avoid over-engineering before understanding what's actually needed.
